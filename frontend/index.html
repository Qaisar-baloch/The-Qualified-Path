How this all fits together

A quick map of the whole process, before you dive into any one section.

01 — The Institution

What is HEC?

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) is Pakistan's regulatory body for higher education. It facilitates dozens of international scholarships for Pakistani/AJ&K students — negotiating slots with foreign governments and universities, then running the shortlisting process domestically.

02 — The Test

What is USAT/HAT?

For most HEC-facilitated scholarships, the first hurdle is a qualifying score on the Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT, for BS-level applicants) or the Higher Education Aptitude Test (HAT, for MS/MPhil-level applicants). Full syllabus and sample papers are in the Test Prep section below.
